In the 90-minute session

Four frameworks you can use on your own product the next morning.

1

Find your sharp wedge

Stop selling to “everyone”. Find the exact buyer with active budget and a cost of inaction you can put a number on.

2

Translate specs into ROI

Rewrite your positioning so finance, operations and procurement see payback — without dumbing down the technology.

3

Prioritise the roadmap ruthlessly

Separate must-have V1 and pilot scope from distracting feature requests, using commercial impact against build effort.

4

Price and package for margin

Why product owns unit economics and packaging — and how to structure product and service tiers without the free-pilot trap.
